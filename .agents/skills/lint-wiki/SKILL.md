---
name: lint-wiki
description: Audit the wiki for stale claims, missing links, contradictions, weak summaries, and pages that need consolidation.
---

# Goal
Keep the wiki coherent as it grows.

# Checklist
- stale pages
- orphan pages
- duplicated topics
- missing cross-links
- contradictions not recorded
- open questions with no next action
- source summaries with weak evidence sections

# Outputs
Update:
- `wiki/dashboards/open-questions.md`
- `wiki/dashboards/contradictions.md`
- `wiki/log.md`
